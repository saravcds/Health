Face Vitals Scanner

Camera-based wellness estimate — heart rate, HRV, respiration & stress

Not a medical device. This tool provides experimental, informational estimates only using ordinary webcam video. It is not clinically validated, must not be used to diagnose or monitor any health condition, and should never replace a real pulse oximeter, ECG, or medical professional. If you have health concerns, please consult a doctor.

How it works

Position your face in the oval, hold still in good, even lighting for about 25 seconds. The app analyzes tiny color changes in your skin caused by blood flow (a technique called remote photoplethysmography, or rPPG) to estimate your vitals — entirely in your browser. No video is uploaded or stored anywhere.

  • Face a light source (window/lamp) — avoid strong backlight
  • Keep your face centered and still during the scan
  • Remove glasses/masks if possible for a cleaner signal
